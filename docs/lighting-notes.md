## Daylight / DMLight (worker: sun + probes) - NOT verified on a device or GPU

**White follower: probable root cause (code reading, no GPU available).** DMLight adds the radiance-cache *bounce* as `emissiveNode` (ui/dm-light.js applyMaterial). Emissive is not masked by the sun shadow, and the cache window is a disc centred on the camera (shader fades `wC`/`wOut` radially round `U.cam`). The bounce was `max(E,0)`: the 4-16 ray EMA estimate is zero-mean noise on flat ground, rectified into a positive mean (test 23 shows ~0.005+ mean), plus real bounce off the mansion/cliffs at full sun intensity. Result: a pale disc that follows the player and fills in sun shadows (which is also why her shadow looked faint). Nothing else positioned at the player is on by day (lamps/LED pools hidden by day, flashlight off until toggled, planet cloud shell already removed in a7755c1).
Fix: `softBounce` (soft threshold, `U.bb`=0.02) + `bounceDayScale` (`U.bk`, eased, 1 at night -> 0.22 at noon). A/B flag: `?lightbounce=0` (off) / `1` default / `>1`. If the wash is still there with `?lightbounce=0`, the cause is NOT the bounce: then try `?light=off`, then `?nopolish`.

**Smooth falloff API (pure, exported in `DMLight.core`, tested in tools/tests/light-unit.js #23):** `smooth01(a,b,v)`, `lampFalloff(d,r)` (1 at lamp, 0 at r, C1), `softBounce(b,bias)`, `bounceDayScale(sunY,day,lo)`, `hyst(state,v,on,off)`. A flashlight/volumetric cone should use `lampFalloff`-style smoothstep for radial fade and `hyst` for on/off; CPU lamp input = `DMLight.setLamps([x,y,z,r,g,b,radius]*n)` + `setLampLevel(0..1)`. Lamp CPU occlusion is now soft (no binary switch-off behind corners).

**Lamp pools (ui/dm-lamps.js):** pool mesh nodes bordering uncovered cells (drops, water, lattice end) now fade to alpha 0 and the radial alpha is smoothstep^2 - no hard cut at ledges.

**Sun shadow:** already texel-snapped, follows the player (DMPolish.fitSun, TERRAIN.sun, PCFSoft, tier map sizes 512-4096, cadence 1-3 frames). Mansion cast shadow is OFF on phones by default (59k-tri pass, ui/dm-facilities.js): flag `?sunshadow=1` enables it on phones. `?sunshadow=0` (read once as `window.DM_SUNSHADOW` in ui/dm-polish.js) restores the old behaviour for the changes made here: contact blob opacity back to 0.52, mansion phone default. It does not touch TERRAIN.sun / fitSun (unchanged old code; `?nopolish` disables fitSun+blob) nor the bounce fix (`?lightbounce`). The cheap contact blob (DMPolish.contact) is stronger (opacity 0.52 -> 0.72) as the phone fallback. Interior (ui/dm-mansion-interior.js) is baked/unlit by design: no real-time shadow indoors.

**Flags:** `?light=off|low|med|high|ultra` (exists), `?lightbounce=0..`, `?sunshadow=0|1`, `?nopolish` (exists; disables fitSun/contact).

## Volumetric fog lighting / DMVLight (worker: light shafts) - NOT verified on a device; shader compiles + renders in SwiftShader only

**What it is.** `ui/dm-vlight.js` (new, sync script after dm-light.js). No screen pass: it installs `scene.fogNode` = stock range fog + single-scattering from a small light list, integrated along the camera->fragment ray in every fogged material. Open sky costs nothing (sky domes have `fog=false`), so a beam shows against terrain/props/walls, NOT against the bare night sky (limitation of doing it in the fog node).
- **Light list** (uniformArray, view space, N = 3/5/8 by tier): `A=(pos,range) B=(rgb*intensity,cosOuter) C=(axis,1/(cosInner-cosOuter))`, axis 0 = point. Top-N by `power/(d^2+4)`, flashlight always first; slots fade in/out 0.25 s (no pop).
- **Integral:** ray clipped to the light's range sphere, equi-angular sampling (`s = tc + H tan a`, K = 2/3/4 jittered samples) with smooth `(1-(r/range)^2)^2` window, spot cone smoothstep, Henyey-Greenstein g=0.45, `exp(-sigma(s+r))`. Point light with window 1 = exact `atan` closed form (tested). Soft knee on the result (never blows out).
- **Medium:** `sigma = density * (haze + mist*exp(-(y-groundY)/mistH)) * patchy`, evaluated at the ray midpoint (local fog/mist density). `DMVLight.setMedium({density:0.02, mist:1, mistH:7, haze:0.18, patchy:0.3, gain:0.5})`; gain is eased down 55 % by day.
- **Sun:** only when low (`sunOn` from elevation, 0 above ~0.55 sin-elevation): 1-2 `DMLight.sunVisNode` samples along the ray modulate a forward-scattered sun term -> shafts where a low sun is occluded. High sun: uniform branch skipped.

**Interface (flashlight worker / anyone).** Default: `tick()` reads `DMFlash.S.light` (SpotLight: position, `S.target` position, angle, penumbra, intensity*color, distance capped at 70 m) when it is in the scene and `intensity > 0` - nothing to do. To own the cone instead: `DMVLight.setSpot('flash', {on, x,y,z, dx,dy,dz (axis, need not be unit), angle (outer half-angle rad), penumbra (0..1), r,g,b (colour*intensity, candela-like), range, gain})` every frame, `DMVLight.clear('flash')` when off. Static lights: `setPoints(key, Float32Array[x,y,z, r,g,b, range]*n, {level, gain})` + `setLevel(key, 0..1)`; wired in ui/dm-lamps.js for `'lamps'` (poles, level = dusk fade) and `'leds'` (balcony rail LEDs). Any new emissive thing = one `setPoints` call. World-space coordinates, no per-frame allocation.

**Flags:** `?vlights=0` (nothing installed), `?vlights=low|med|high` forces a tier (default: phones/coarse pointer = low, desktop follows DMLight tier), `?vlgain=` (default 0.5), `?vldens=` (default 0.02 /m). Hooks in index.html: `DMVLight.install(scene,{THREE})` right after `scene.fog` is created (installing later would recompile every fogged material once), `DMVLight.tick(...)` right after `DMLight.tick`.

**Perf / budget:** per fogged pixel: one uniform branch (off = free), else N lights x (clip test, 1 atan x2, K samples of tan/sqrt/exp/pow). Light with no overlap with the ray early-outs. Budget: `DMBudget.lvl('light')` 1 -> half the lights, 2 -> 2 lights + no sun, 3 (or emaMs > 45/60) -> fade to off (1.5 s ramp), recovers the same way; all uniform writes, no recompile. `uMaxD` 120 m caps the integrated path, sun 260 m.

**Tests:** `node tools/tests/vlight-unit.js` (packing, picker, slot fading, window/cone smoothness, point + spot vs 40k-step brute force: median 1.5 % / worst 13 % rel err at K=4 in narrow-window cases, atan closed form 1e-8, finite on 4000 random rays, budget plan). `tools/tests/vlight-harness.html` renders the real fogNode (`?gl=1&side=1&vlights=high&sun=1`); it built and rendered with no errors on WebGL2/SwiftShader for low/med/high + sun. Colours/gains are untuned guesses.

**Open issues:** (1) beams are invisible against bare sky/background by design; (2) K=3 jitter shows faint grain at the cone edge on large flat ground (IGN noise, amplitude 0.6/K) - raise tier or soften the spot penumbra; (3) DMLight sun visibility needs `DMLight.on` + a march tier, else the low-sun term is un-occluded haze toward the sun; (4) dm-atmo.js (planet-scale gas, units of planet radii) and dm-mist.js (asteroid fume sprites in space, `fog=false`) are deliberately NOT lit by local lamps - metres vs planet radii; fog-true materials get shafts automatically; (5) the lamps/LED intensities (380/235/114 @22 m, 70/58/42 @7 m) are guesses to tune on a device with `?vlgain=`; (6) scene fog is now owned by the fogNode: anything that replaces `scene.fog` with a different object is followed via `tick` (colour/near/far copied each frame), a scene without `scene.fog` gets `fogNode = null`.

**DMVLight teardown (QA finding):** `S.scene` removed (it kept the last world scene alive); `DMVLight.release(scene)` is called from `_pwClose` (drops `scene.fogNode`, fades out, clears slots and the auto flash spot). tick() no longer remembers scenes.

## Flashlight spot + shadows + beam (ui/dm-flash.js) - flashlight worker

Nothing below ran on a GPU. Node tests build the real WGSL with a stub renderer (tools/tests/flash-light.js part 2) but no frame was ever rendered.

### How it works
- One `SpotLight` per scene she can be in (`flightScene`, each world scene), created by `DMFlash.adoptScene` before the first compile, intensity 0 while off. Tagged `userData.dmFlash`. Every lit material gets it through three's own light loop (terrain, props, character, board, foliage: they all use the scene light list).
- Materials with a PRIVATE light list needed one hook each: mansion atlas (`ui/dm-mansion-look.js` `lightList(ctx, true)`, kept out of the probe-scaled list) and Bird Temple hull/interior (`ui/dm-bt-lighting.js` `lightSets`, via `DMFlash.sceneLight(flightScene)`). The `_lcNode` world lists (index.html) already include every non-excluded scene light.
- DMLight: `m.receivedShadowNode` wraps EVERY light's shadow node (sun-box fade + sun height-field visibility). That would have dimmed the spot at night / outside the sun box, so the spot's shadow node is tagged `dmFlashShadow` and the 4 wrapper branches in `ui/dm-light.js` return it untouched. Nothing else in DMLight changed (AO only touches indirect light; the spot is direct light, so it is not dimmed by day sun, sky visibility or AO).
- Shadow: `light.shadow.shadowNode` = a `ShadowNode` subclass whose PCF filter sits in `If(U.shOn > 0.5)`: when the flashlight is off (or shadows are throttled off) the pixel shader skips the map taps. `shadow.autoUpdate=false`; `tick()` sets `needsUpdate` on the tier's cadence. `shadow.updateMatrices` is wrapped so the shadow far plane = `min(tier.far, mode.shFar, light.distance)` (the light keeps its 260 m reach, casters beyond the shadow reach are never drawn into the map; beyond it the surface is lit unshadowed). Log-depth safe (the project's renderer uses a logarithmic depth buffer; three's `setupShadowCoord` has a branch for it, bias is added in log-depth units).
- Beam: one additive back-face cone mesh per scene (`jots_flash_beam`, renderOrder 52, hidden while off). Fragment shader: analytic ray/solid-cone interval (`DMFlash.coneInterval`, brute-force tested), stopped by the scene depth (same technique as `_pwAtmoShell`), 6/8/14 samples (tier), interleaved-gradient jitter, density x angular smoothstep (penumbra) x length fade x near falloff x Henyey-Greenstein forward phase, desktop adds `mx_noise_float` dust. **Yields to DMVLight**: while `DMVLight.on` and the scene has a `fogNode` (on foot / flying) the cone hides itself so the shaft is not counted twice (DMVLight already scatters `DMFlash.S.light` through the fog; it reads `S.light`/`S.target`, which the spot pairs keep current). The cone is the beam in open space, in fog-less scenes and with `?vlights=0`; `?flashbeam=<k>` forces it on top. No beam when the scene depth cannot be bound (multisampled WebGPU, `_viewportDepthOK()`), because it would pour through the ground.

### Interface for the other passes
- `DMFlash.cone()` (preallocated, valid after `tick`): `{on, mode, pos{x,y,z}, dir{x,y,z}, angle, penumbra, cosOuter, cosInner, range, intensity, lumens, cone, shadow{on, light, map, matrix, near, far, bias, size, every}}`.
- `DMFlash.U` (TSL uniform nodes, `null` until the first spot is made on a TSL build): `A` apex, `a` axis, `cosO`, `cosI`, `len` (visible shaft length), `k` (beam gain, 0 when off), `dens`, `col`, `shOn`. A fog / mist / atmosphere pass can read these to add its own in-scatter.
- `DMFlash.medium = {mul, add}`: scale/offset of the beam's air density (per metre). Base: 0.010 on the ground x clamp(520/fog.far, 0.5, 3); 0.0006 in space. A volumetric-light pass that knows the real fog/mist density should set these instead of guessing from `scene.fog`.
- `DMFlash.sceneLight(scene)`, `DMFlash.hooks = {gfx, depthOK, logDepth}` (set in index.html before the first `adoptScene`).

### Tiers (`?flashtier=low|phone|desktop`, else from GFX / UA)
| tier | shadow map | refresh | shadow reach | beam samples | noise |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| low (Low preset / shadowSize<=512) | 512 | every 2nd frame | 60 m | 6 | no |
| phone (isMobile) | 1024 | every 2nd frame | 100 m | 8 | no |
| desktop | 2048 | every frame | 180 m | 14 | yes |
Also: map side capped by `GFX.shadowSize`; `DMBudget.shadowEvery()` / `shadowHalf()` honoured; frame-time EMA > 38 ms -> every 2nd, > 55 ms -> every 3rd, > 72 ms for 90 frames -> shadows off (cone + light stay), retried after ~10 s of < 42 ms (2 retries, then off for the session). Space mode: light reach 1500 units, shadow range 140 units.

### Flags
`?flashlight=0` (no light created at all, button stays) - `?flashshadow=0|512|1024|2048` - `?flashbeam=auto|0|k` - `?flashtier=` - `?flashbias=-0.0004`. Session only; nothing in localStorage. Beam width: long-press the light button or tap the cone key atop the brightness bar (wide 35 deg / medium 23 / spot 12; same lumens, narrower = brighter).

### Approximations / known gaps
- The visible shaft is NOT occluded by objects inside the cone (only by the scene depth). Real shadow-map-occluded shafts would need the spot's depth map bound into the beam shader.
- Shadow map refresh at half rate on phones: a fast head turn drags the shadow edge for a frame.
- The spot is evaluated (direct BRDF) for every lit fragment even at intensity 0 (as before); only the shadow taps are branch-gated. `?flashlight=0` removes even that.
- Sampled-texture budget: each shadow-RECEIVING material now binds one more depth texture (+ sampler). If a material is already at WebGPU's 16 sampled textures per stage (terrain with DMLight cascades + sun shadow + atlases is the suspect) its pipeline will fail to create. Unverified. Kill switch: `?flashshadow=0`. Suspect to check first on a device: terrain/ground atlas materials with `userData.dmLight`.
- Shadow bias/normalBias (-0.0004 / 0.06 m) are first guesses for a log-depth 1024-2048 map; tune with `?flashbias=`.
- Tests: `node tools/tests/glasses-flash.js`, `node tools/tests/flash-light.js` (set THREE_CORE, optionally THREE_WEBGPU).

## Lamps / dusk / her shadow / real lamp lights (worker: lamps + shadows) - NOT verified on a device or WebGPU

Nothing here ran on a phone or on the WebGPU backend. Ran: node tests (mansion-lamps.js, home-leds.js, lighting-budget.js, light-unit.js) and one WebGL2/SwiftShader render (tools/tests/lamp-shadow-harness.html: a tagged point shadow lights a floor through a DMLight-style wrapper and the box shadows it).

### "Some weird lamp following me" - what it was (found with a SwiftShader A/B, NOT confirmed on the tablet)
Most likely culprit: **the low-sun in-scatter of DMVLight** (ui/dm-vlight.js `sunTerm`, new in v319). It adds `sunCol * HG(sun.view, g=0.7) * (1 - exp(-sigma L)) * sunVis` to every fogged fragment whenever the sun is below ~33 deg (it is on at dusk). HG with g = 0.7 is a ~25 deg wide glow round the sun's direction, so it is VIEW-attached: on the ground 5-25 m from the camera it is a pale patch facing the sun, bounded by the lobe (curved) and by the occluders' straight edges through `sunVis`, and it moves with where she looks / walks, with the flashlight off, strongest at dusk. tools/tests/vlight-harness.html `?gl=1&vlights=high&sun=1&flash=0&lamps=0` shows it (floor patch, mean luminance 8.5) and with the fix it is gone (4.2); Fix: the term is multiplied by `smoothstep(SUN_NEAR0=30, SUN_NEAR1=110, viewDistance)^2` - nothing within ~30 m of the camera, full only on far terrain (aerial perspective is what it is for). Unit-tested (`sunNearK`).
Ruled out / checked: (a) the flashlight (intensity is exactly 0 while off: dm-flash.js sync); (b) DMNatLights / `TERRAIN.natPool` (2 pooled lights, only the kite-charm and real fires request them; intensity 0 otherwise); (c) the lamp light pools (ui/dm-lamps.js): static world meshes that cannot follow; but their border fade was only ONE cell wide, so a pool that ended at a rail / ledge read as a hard-edged lit shape - now a smoothstep over 3 cells (`EDGE_CELLS`); (d) the DMLight radiance cache: bounce was already softened in v319; a fixed-camera A/B (tools/tests/follow-harness.html: only the player focus given to `DMLight.tick` changes, camera and world fixed, every differing pixel is player-attached) shows exactly ONE player-attached term left in the material hook: the sun shadow map's fade disc (`wMap`, `DMLight.setBands`): inside it map shadows apply (x the height-field term), outside they are absent; it is a smoothstep and ~14/255 at most in the harness, switching bounce / AO / height-field off does not remove it (only `sm` far does). If a pale shape is STILL there after v321: `?vlights=0` (DMVLight off) first, then `?lightbounce=0`, then `?light=off`, then `?nopolish`, then `?lamplights=0` - one of them removes it and tells us which.

### Dusk ramp (ui/dm-mansion-rooms.js `duskLevel/duskStep`, wired in ui/dm-lamps.js tick, ctx `skySunY` in index.html)
The lamps used `day = clamp((sunY+0.1)/0.25)`, which is already 1 from 8.6 deg up, so nothing could start before ~3 deg. Now the SUN ELEVATION drives one level `S.on`: 0 at/above 14 deg, smoothstep, 1 at/below 0 deg (`?dusk=<deg>` moves the start). Hysteresis: the elevation the ramp sees only moves once the sun has left a +-0.35 deg dead band (a jittering sun cannot flicker a lamp; a noisy sunset never dims a lamp that came on; tested). `S.on` (eased 0.9 s) feeds EVERYTHING: pole heads + glow + pools, rail LEDs, `DMLight.setLampLevel`, `DMVLight.setLevel('lamps'|'leds')` and the real lamp lights below. Room switches / interior AUTO levels still use `day` (unchanged).

### Real lamp lights, restored (ui/dm-lamps.js `lampPlan`, `lampLights`)
History (git log -S): there never were shadowed real lamp lights in this repo. The only real lights were `?lamplights=1..4` (since 4b4a0ce): UNshadowed PointLights, OFF by default because adding a light recompiles every lit material. They are now ON by DMLight tier, still created ONCE (at lamp build) and never added/removed afterwards:
| DMLight tier | lights | shadow-casting | face | refresh | memory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| off / low | 0 (probe lighting from the radiance cache only) | 0 | - | - | 0 |
| med (the phone ceiling, `PRESET_CAP.mob`) | 2 | 1 | 512 | every 3rd frame | 8 MiB |
| high | 3 | 2 | 512 | every 2nd | 16 MiB |
| ultra | 4 | 3 | 1024 | every frame | 96 MiB (desktop) |
A point light's depth atlas is 4 x 2 faces, so "512" = 2048 x 1024 x 4 B. Softness = `shadow.radius` 1.5 / 2 / 3 on the renderer's PCF type. Casters: her (skinned mesh casts), lamp poles (`castShadow` only when a lamp casts), props that already cast; the mansion still casts nothing on phones (59k tris x 6 faces) - `?sunshadow=1` makes it cast for the sun AND the lamps.
Slots: a slot keeps its lamp until that lamp leaves the nearest set, then fades out (0.3 s), then moves (no pops); re-aimed 3x a second from the mansion frame; by day / > 150 m from the property nothing is assigned, intensity 0, no map refreshed. Maps are `autoUpdate=false`, refreshed on the cadence and only for slots with light.
DMBudget (no new ladder stage, no recompile): `shadow` L1 -> cadence >= 2, L2 (or `light` L2) -> ONE shadowed lamp, L3 (or `light` L3) -> maps frozen and at most 2 lamps lit, L4 -> frozen. `castShadow` is NEVER toggled at runtime (that recompiles).
The point shadow node is a tagged subclass (`lampShadowNode`: `PointShadowNode.setupShadow` -> `dmFlashShadow = true`) so DMLight's `receivedShadowNode` wrapper (sun-box fade x SUN height-field visibility) does not multiply a lamp's shadow by the sun's visibility (it would be ~0 at night). Verified on WebGL2: an untagged light IS zeroed by such a wrapper, the tagged one is not.
Flags: `?lamplights=0` (none) / `=N` (count), `?lampshadow=0` (unshadowed lights) / `=<face px>`, `?lamptier=low|med|high|ultra` (override), `?lampk=<gain>` (intensity, default 1 = 80 cd, decay 1.5, 32 m), `?dusk=<deg>`.
**Texture slots (flashlight worker: read this).** Each shadow-casting lamp adds one depth texture (+ comparison sampler) to every shadow-RECEIVING lit material in the world scene, on top of the sun map and the flashlight map. At med that is sun + flash + 1 lamp. The terrain ground material (DMLight cascades + atlases) is the suspect for the 16-per-stage limit; if terrain / props go black or pink on a device, `?lampshadow=0` is the kill switch (keeps the lights, drops their maps), then `?lamplights=0`. tools/tests/lighting-budget.js check 10 prints the slots per tier and fails if the phone ceiling (med) has > 1 shadowed lamp.

### Her shadow outside (index.html fitSun call, ui/dm-facilities.js)
Two causes found by code reading: (1) the mansion's EXTERIOR meshes got `receiveShadow` only together with `castShadow`, i.e. never on phones/tablets, so the sun's / lamps' shadow of her could not land on the balcony, decks or floors at all. Cast and receive are now decoupled (receive on whenever shadows are on, `?sunshadow=0` restores the old all-or-nothing). (2) The sun's shadow direction fell back to a fixed high light (80,120,40) as soon as the sun was below ~3 deg, i.e. through all of dusk; it now follows the sun down to -4.6 deg clamped to 3.4 deg elevation. The soft contact blob (DMPolish.contact) is unchanged and remains the phone fallback. The interior is baked/unlit (no real-time shadow indoors, by design).

## Flashlight v322: "the flashlight still does not light the scene" (ui/dm-flash.js) - flashlight worker

What was reported (tablet, Solenne mansion balcony, dusk): flashlight ON at max, ground in front dark except a pale fan-shaped patch with hard straight edges and an arc edge, walls / columns not lit. NOT verified on a device. Everything below was checked with (a) node tests on the real TSL/WGSL (tools/tests/flash-light.js, 164 checks) and (b) a real render of the real `ui/dm-flash.js` spot on three 0.185 WebGPURenderer's WebGL2 backend in headless Chromium/SwiftShader (tools/tests/flash-render.py: log depth, ACES, PCF-soft shadows; ground / wall / columns / custom colorNode material are lit, column shadows fall on the wall).

**Root causes found (code + real WGSL + render):**
1. **Shadow lookup only existed on the FIRST material that compiled.** `shadowNodeFor` wrote its `If`/`toVar` straight into `setupShadowFilter`. `ShadowNode` caches its output (`_node`) and reuses it for every material's builder, but statements (`If`, `assign`) live on the *stack of the builder that created them*: every later material got `out = 1` and no `textureGatherCompare` at all (checked: 19 KB WGSL with the gather for the first mesh, none for the 2nd / 3rd). So the spot had a shadow on one random surface and none on the rest. Fix: the branch is now an `Fn(...)()` call node (an expression), checked per family in the node test.
2. **Shadow bias was a constant, the receiver is the ground at grazing angles.** The spot is at head height; a shadow texel covers ~0.14 % of the distance across and ~10x that along the ground, so a fixed log-depth bias + 6 cm normalBias self-shadows the ground (acne = dark) beyond ~10-15 m, while the band behind the shadow far plane (100 m phone) is outside the map and fully lit - a lit "sector" with an ARC edge at the shadow far plane. Fix: `setupShadowCoord` pulls the receiver toward the light by `biasK x distance + biasM` (1.2 % + 3 cm, `?flashbiask=`), same log-depth maths as three otherwise; the shadow eases out over the last 45 % of its reach (no lit/shadowed arc); `shadow.intensity = 0.9` so a shadow failure can never black a surface out.
3. **Aim.** The beam axis was the glasses' forward = horizontal at 1.6 m: the wide cone's lower edge meets the ground ~2.3 m ahead at a grazing angle, so the footprint of a horizontal torch on flat ground is a thin straight-edged WEDGE (the cone / ground conic looks like a fan), and the ground in front of her is never inside the cone. On foot the axis is now tilted 10 deg down (`?flashtilt=deg`, 0 = old), in space it is untouched. The render harness shows the near ground lit with the tilt, a wedge without it.
4. **Mansion atlas used a closed world's lights.** `dm-mansion-look.js` built the atlas's private light list once per session (`S.atlasLights === undefined`), but every planet world is a new scene with its own ambient/hemisphere lights and its own flashlight spot: from the second visit the atlas lit itself with the old world's lights (spot removed + disposed, intensity 0). The list is now rebuilt when the light objects differ (identity compare, at the same point as the existing `needsUpdate`).

**Not the cause (checked):** intensity / exposure (1400 cd max is blown out at 3-10 m in the render; default step 2 = 700 cd); the beam cone mesh (soft in the render, hidden while DMVLight is on a fogged scene); other material families (all are `MeshStandard/Physical/Lambert` node materials that run three's light loop; the only private lists are the mansion atlas and the Bird Temple hull / interior, both include the spot). The **pale wedge** that was seen is most likely DMVLight's in-scatter of the spot in the fog (`range = min(L.distance, 70)` gives the arc, the cone gives the straight edges) plus the thin ground footprint of cause 3, i.e. the shaft was visible but the surfaces were not lit by it.

**`?flashdebug=1`** prints (console, 2.5 s after switch-on and every 15 s while on) which material families RECEIVE the spot: `[flashdebug] LIT|BLIND xN <Type "name"> - why`. BLIND = unlit type (MeshBasic*, ShaderMaterial, Points, Sprite, `lights=false`), a private `lightsNode` list without THIS scene's spot (and it says when it holds another scene's spot), or a spot that is not a child of the drawn scene. `DMFlash.audit(scene)` / `DMFlash.receives(material, spot, scene)` are the pure functions. MeshBasic* families (sky, water FX, glows, mist puffs) are unlit on purpose and stay BLIND.

**Sampled-texture budget (unverified on a device).** Each shadow-RECEIVING material binds one more depth texture + comparison sampler for the spot. The node test asserts <= 16 textures / <= 16 samplers for the plain / colorNode / DMLight-wrapped / physical / Lambert / atlas / hull families (they bind 2-3), but the real terrain (DMLight cascades + ground atlas + sun shadow) can only be counted on a device: `DMFlash.status()` + `?flashshadow=0` is the kill switch; if the terrain pipeline fails on a device its console error names `maxSampledTexturesPerShaderStage`.

Flags added: `?flashdebug=1`, `?flashtilt=<deg>`, `?flashbiask=<k>`. Tests: `node tools/tests/flash-light.js` (THREE_CORE + THREE_WEBGPU), `python3 tools/tests/flash-render.py <three-dir> [outdir]` (needs PIL + headless Chromium; ~20 s).

### What the lit circle was
Reproduced in the render harness at the old defaults (v323, max step): NOT a separate artefact, no extra mesh. It is the old cone's footprint made visible by clipping: 35 deg half-angle, three's smoothstep penumbra, 1/d^1.5 decay and a 700-1400 cd output gave 20-60 lux at 3-10 m, i.e. far above the ACES white point (lum 230-250 on ground / wall / cube). Everything above ~10 % of the peak is clipped to white, so the soft smoothstep tail is only visible as a thin, steep edge: a hard-looking white oval, then black (dusk ambient only, `exp` of the grazing cosine on a floor makes the ground fall off as ~d^-2.5, ~15 m). ### New profile (one extra analytic term inside three's SpotLightNode, no second shadow map, one shadow caster)
`SpotLightNode.prototype.getSpotAttenuation` is wrapped once (`DMFlash.patch`, before the first compile; only lights with `userData.dmFlash`; other spots untouched): `window(smoothstep cone) x (exp(-(1-c)/(1-cos 11deg)) + 0.55 x exp(-(1-c)/(1-cos 34deg))) x (1+(d/12)^2)^-0.85 x 0.013` (c = cos of the angle to the axis). Hot Gaussian core + wide soft halo, window to 54 deg (zero there, ~1 per mille of the core: no perimeter), distance law flat to ~8 m and ~d^-1.7 beyond. Uniforms only (`DMFlash.U.kh/kw/wh/id0/dp/cal/sp`); `sp` = 0 in space keeps the legacy cone + decay. `F.spotFactor()` is the pure mirror (tested: smoothness 0.25 deg steps < 3 % of the peak, monotonic, halo 35 deg = 8-40 % of the core, 40 m / 10 m = 0.04-0.2).
- Calibrated lux on a surface facing her at the default step (3000 lm = 1050 cd): core 13 lux at 10 m, 2.4 at 40 m, 1.2 at 60 m; halo at 30 deg ~2 lux at 10 m. Max step is ~1.3x that (the hot core clips, the halo does not). Default step is now 3000 lm (was 2000).
- Cone presets scale both lobes: wide 11/34 deg sigma (54 deg window), medium x0.66, spot x0.4 (still gain 1 / 1.35 / 2.1).
- Aim: default tilt 4 deg down (was 10): walls, columns and distant surfaces are lit, the floor ahead gets the halo. `?flashtilt=deg`.
- Shadow: the shadow camera uses `focus 0.78` (86 deg fov instead of 108): the outer halo past ~43 deg is lit but unshadowed (the shadow lookup returns lit outside the map), which is the cheap part. Floor = 10 % of the light survives in shadow (unchanged), so a shadow failure still never blacks a surface out.
- Detail beyond the hot core (item 2): checked, nothing clamped. The spot is direct light through the standard BRDF: normal maps / textures get N.L like any light; there is no ambient clamp and no extra tone mapping (flight renderer = ACES, exposure 1.0). ### Frame rate: where the lighting stack costs (code analysis; no GPU profile, estimates are relative per-frame GPU cost on a Mali / iPad class tablet at ~0.4-1 MP)
Ranked by estimated cost while the flashlight is on at the Solenne mansion at dusk:
1. DMVLight fog node: runs for EVERY fogged pixel (all terrain / props / mansion) as soon as `U.k > 0`; it used to be 1 all the time on foot (even by day, no light near): N lights x K samples x (atan x2, tan, sqrt, exp, pow) + at dusk 1-2 sun-visibility shadow taps. Biggest single item (est. 20-40 % of frame at dusk on a tablet).
2. Sun shadow map (2048-4096 tier, existing DMBudget cadence) and the lamp point shadows (2 x 6 faces, other worker).
3. Flashlight shadow map: was 1024 / 100 m on tablets every 2nd frame; every shadow-receiving fragment does a PCF-soft lookup (gated by a uniform while off).
4. Spot BRDF is evaluated for every lit fragment even while off (no way to skip it without a recompile): now also 3 `exp`/1 `pow` more per fragment (~2-4 % ALU, est.).
5. DMLight radiance cache (own DMBudget stages).

Changes made (all uniform writes / cadences, no recompile on toggle; the spot and its map exist from adoptScene as before):
- **DMVLight only pays when something lights it**: gather first, `want` = flashlight / lamp / explicit light in reach OR the low sun on. By day with no light in reach and a high sun the fog node's branch is off (free). Est. saving: the whole fog-node cost (est. 15-35 % of frame time) whenever no lamp / flashlight is near, i.e. all day and away from the mansion.
- **Low tier (phones / tablets) has no low-sun in-scatter** (`?vlsun=1` brings it back): removes the 1-2 sun-visibility taps per fogged pixel at dusk and the view-attached glow that was the likely "follower". Est. 5-10 % at dusk.
- **Flash tiers**: phone / tablet 512 map (was 1024: 1/4 the fill), every 2nd frame, 80 m reach (was 100), 6 beam samples (was 8); low preset 512, every 3rd, 45 m, 5 samples. Desktop unchanged. Est. flashlight shadow pass -50..70 % on tablets; the soft 512 shadow reads the same under a 54 deg halo.
- **DMBudget fast guard** (`DMBudget.fast()`, 0..3; ui/dm-budget.js `createFast`): the existing ladder sheds the lighting stack last (light 1 at p8, shadows p14+) one step per >= 1 s dwell; this separate guard watches the same frame times: EMA < 20 fps for 2 s -> +1 (again every 2 s), > 30 fps for 6 s -> -1; a 30 fps-locked device never sheds (tested), hitches > 1.5 s are not samples. Consumers: DMFlash (1: shadow map 512 + every 3rd frame; 2: flashlight shadows off, light stays; 3: shaft cone off) and DMVLight (fast = at least that `light` level: 1 half the lights, 2 two lights + no sun, 3 off with a 1.5 s ramp). From 5 fps the lights are fully shed in ~6 s (was: ladder stage 14-17, 15-30 s). `?govfast=0` disables. Other workers (lamp shadows, DMLight) can read `DMBudget.fast()`.
- DMFlash's own governor (38 / 55 / 72 ms) is unchanged as the second line of defence.

<h2 align="center">Features</h2>

<div align="center">

- Zero-G interstellar flight
- Build your skyboard and fly the stars
- Chart the galaxy and ride at c³
- WebGPU in the browser, with a WebGL fallback
- Installable PWA — play at [dartmeadow.space](https://dartmeadow.space/)

</div>
